## 7. Content and legal-trust guardrails

The client said service fees should be described as applicable government fees that can change or be confirmed on request. Recommended plain-language wording:

> **Government fees:** Applicable government fees are additional and may change under the relevant rules or notifications. We will confirm the applicable amount for your application before proceeding. Professional service charges, if any, will be communicated separately.

Adapt the wording after a qualified reviewer checks the actual fee/payment model. Do not imply that all services have the same government fee.

- Trademark filing does not guarantee acceptance or registration.
- Do not guarantee outcomes, fixed registration timelines or “100% success”.
- Clearly distinguish BharatMarks' service fees from government charges.
- Explain scope, exclusions, taxes if applicable, payment timing and refund/cancellation rules once the client confirms them. Do not invent a refund policy.

During design, use clearly labelled internal placeholders such as **[Insert approved customer testimonial]** and keep them unpublished until replaced with real, permissioned testimonials.
