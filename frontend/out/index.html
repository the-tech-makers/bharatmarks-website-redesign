How It Works

A clear path from idea to registered trademark

Everything happens online. You always know which stage your application is at — and what happens next.

  1. 01Before you file

    Trademark Search

    We investigate whether similar trademarks already exist, minimising future issues before you invest in the brand.

  2. 02Official filing

    Drafting & Filing

    We handle the official process of submitting your application to the Trade Marks Registry, with the right classes selected.

  3. 03When concerns arise

    Objection & Hearing

    If the Registry raises concerns, we craft responses to address them and represent you at hearings along the way.

  4. 04Every 10 years

    Registration & Renewal

    Once registered, we track renewal deadlines for you so your protection never quietly lapses.

Timelines vary by application — trademark registration in India typically takes 12 to 18 months, depending on objections or challenges that may arise. We keep you informed throughout.
