# BharatMarks Homepage Visual Direction

**Status:** Proposed design direction for review  
**Brand asset:** https://bharatmarks.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Bharatmarsk_logoforweb_bluewithGANDO.png  
**Audience:** Indian founders and businesses seeking trademark services in the USA, UK, EU, and Australia.

## Design principles

- Keep the existing BharatMarks logo; do not redesign the identity as part of the website refresh.
- Build a clean, premium, approachable international professional-services experience.
- Use deep navy/blue as the primary brand color, white and pale blue-grey surfaces, and restrained orange/green accents sampled from the logo after inspecting the source asset.
- Use generous whitespace, a readable sans-serif type system, consistent line lengths, clear focus states, and responsive layouts.
- Avoid generic gavels, courthouse imagery, cluttered promotional banners, and unsupported claims.

## Homepage structure

1. **Header:** Existing logo, Services, Destinations, About, Resources, Contact, and a prominent “Get a Quote” CTA. Mobile navigation must be keyboard accessible.
2. **Hero:** “Protect Your Brand in the USA, UK, EU and Australia.” Supporting copy should explain that BharatMarks helps Indian businesses navigate international trademark search, registration, and related support. Primary CTA: “Get an International Trademark Quote.” Secondary CTA: “Explore Services.”
3. **Destination selector:** USA, UK, EU, Australia, each linked to a dedicated landing page.
4. **Trust and service approach:** Explain the process, communication, fee transparency, and coordination with appropriately qualified professionals where required. Publish only verified credentials and service claims.
5. **Services:** Trademark search, registration, renewal, objection/examination response, hearing, assignment/transfer. Use service names only where currently offered and confirmed.
6. **Destination cards:** Four distinct pages and country/region-specific procedures. Make clear that trademark rights are territorial and the EU and UK are separate jurisdictions.
7. **How it works:** Enquiry and requirements → scope/quote → case-specific search or filing and professional coordination → updates and next steps. Do not promise registration or a fixed outcome.
8. **Evidence and testimonials:** Add only genuine, attributable, permissioned testimonials and verified qualifications. Until approved evidence is available, omit testimonials rather than publish invented reviews.
9. **FAQs:** Answer common questions with legally reviewed, jurisdiction-specific information and official sources.
10. **Final enquiry CTA and footer:** Verified phone, email, WhatsApp, privacy policy, terms, disclaimer, and useful service links.

## Content and legal guardrails

- Separate professional fees, government fees, and applicable taxes. State that official fees may change and confirm the current fee for each matter; do not invent prices or refund terms.
- Do not imply BharatMarks itself is a licensed law firm or that its staff hold destination-country credentials unless verified.
- Explain that foreign-domiciled US applicants generally need a US-licensed attorney, subject to current USPTO rules.
- Never guarantee registration, approval, enforcement outcomes, or processing dates.
- Use real client proof only with permission; no fabricated reviews, client logos, awards, or success statistics.
- Use plain English and explain legal terminology.

## Implementation and QA notes

- Treat this as a visual direction, not production-ready copy or a final legal review.
- Inspect the actual logo file for transparency, dimensions, and colors before setting final CSS tokens.
- Confirm current WordPress theme, builder, plugins, analytics, Search Console, URLs, and hosting before implementation.
- Preserve valuable URLs and rankings; create a redirect map before any URL changes.
- Test mobile navigation, forms, keyboard access, contrast, focus, validation, spam controls, and enquiry delivery.
- Obtain approval from the business owner and an appropriately qualified legal reviewer before launch.
